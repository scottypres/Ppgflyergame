Paramotor Hustle

Run tandem flights. Build your reputation. Make that money.

How to Play

The Goal

You're a tandem paramotor pilot building your business over a season. Earn money, build your reputation, and upgrade your gear.

Each Day

Choose one of three actions:

  • Fly Tandems — Customers will approach you. Set your price and watch them decide. Good weather and reputation bring more customers.
  • Visit Market — Travel to a fly-in market. Buy fuel, upgrade your wing, motor, or accessories. Prices change every visit.
  • Rest — Take the day off. Your reputation drops slightly as people forget about you.

Tips

  • Watch the weather — calm, smooth days are best for flying.
  • Don't run out of fuel!
  • Better gear means happier passengers and more tips.
  • Balance flying and shopping for the best score.
